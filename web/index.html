ClipFinder

Calibrating Recording

    Find your Call Of Duty Mobile Multiplayer clips

    ClipFinder finds every multi-kill streak in your COD Mobile Multiplayer match recording and gives you the option to save each one as a clip, ready for editing in any application. It’s free, there’s no sign-up, and your video never leaves your device.

    How to find your clips

    1. Click on “Select Match Recording” and pick your Multiplayer(MP) video recording .
    2. Choose how it was recorded: a live match recording or a replay mode recording.
    3. Pick the minimum number of kills a clip needs. For example: selecting 4 will find all the clips with 4 kills or more.
    4. Click “Find clips” and keep this page open while it scans. When it’s done, you can view each clip in a preview window and download the ones you want.

    Notes

    • Works fastest/best in Chrome or Edge.
    • Clips are cut from your original video, so they keep its original quality.
    • Clips are listed in order of a "score" assigned to them, prioritizing high kills done in a short amount.
    • The reader may misread or miss kills here and there and is *NOT* 100% accurate, incase you run into any such misreadings or are unable to calibrate your recordings, reach out to me in the Contact section.
